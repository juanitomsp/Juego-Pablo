**El Pablo has no accounts, no ads and does not collect personal data.**

## What is stored on your phone

- Your **nickname** and a **random identifier**, so you can get back to your seat if the connection drops.
- Your **statistics** (games played, won, tricks…), your daily challenges and the live scoreboard.

All of this stays **only on your device**: it is not sent anywhere. It is deleted by uninstalling the app or from **Statistics → Delete data from this browser**.

## What happens when you play online

- While you are in a room, the server knows your **nickname** and random identifier so you can play, only **in memory** and only while the room exists. The other players at the table (and anyone watching it) see your nickname. The list of public games does not show nicknames.
- **Chat messages** are passed on to the players at your table at that moment and are **not stored**.
- **Reports** of other players are only counted inside the room, to remove a player when enough people at the table report them. Nothing is stored about who reported whom; the server only keeps an anonymous monthly total of reports.
- To prevent abuse, the server uses your **IP address** for a few minutes, in memory, to limit connections and attempts. It is not stored.
- The server counts **how many people play** (distinct players, games and hands per day and per month) **anonymously**: it only stores totals, without names, identifiers or IPs.
